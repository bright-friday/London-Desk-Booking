# User Onboarding Guide

## Getting Started

1. Access the desk booking application
2. Log in with your credentials
3. Complete your profile setup

## How to Book a Desk

1. Click "New Booking"
2. Select date and time
3. Choose available desk
4. Confirm booking
5. Check email for confirmation

## Check-in Process

1. Arrive at desk
2. Scan QR code or click check-in button
3. System confirms your presence

## Cancellation

1. Go to "My Bookings"
2. Select booking to cancel
3. Click "Cancel Booking"
4. Note: Cancellations must be made 2+ hours before booking
